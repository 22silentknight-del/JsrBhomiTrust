About the Trust

JSR Devbhoomi Shiksha Trust was founded to promote education and public benefit through educational institutions and programmes, scholarships, libraries, research, skill development, preservation of Uttarakhand’s heritage, environmental education and related charitable activities.

Why we exist

The Settlor wished to establish a public charitable and educational trust dedicated to learning, knowledge, research, heritage, languages and literature, and to the educational and social development of Devbhoomi.

How it is held

The Settlor has irrevocably dedicated the initial corpus to the Trust. The Trustees have agreed to administer the Trust Fund in accordance with the Deed and applicable law.

Non-profit by design

Income and property are applied solely towards the charitable objects and lawful administrative expenses, and never distributed as private profit.

Our objectives

The Trust Deed sets out these objects. Open any area to read what the Trust is empowered to do.

Promotion of education
  • Promote and support primary, secondary, higher, adult, continuing, non-formal, distance and vocational education, subject to recognition and regulatory requirements.
  • Establish, run or support schools, colleges, learning and study centres, coaching centres, vocational institutes, skill centres and hostels, subject to law and approvals.
  • Provide scholarships, stipends, grants, books, uniforms, stationery, computers, assistive devices and digital learning facilities to deserving students.
  • Promote inclusive education, equal access to learning, adult literacy and innovative teaching methods.
  • Support teacher training, educator development, curriculum improvement and educational leadership.
  • Offer educational guidance, career counselling, exam preparation, mentoring and academic support to young people.
Devbhoomi Uttarakhand: heritage and culture
  • Promote education and research on Uttarakhand’s history, geography, languages, literature, folk traditions, arts, crafts and architecture.
  • Document, preserve and share folk songs, folk dances, oral histories, manuscripts, local languages and folklore.
  • Build public awareness of Uttarakhand’s historical, cultural and linguistic significance and its place in India’s heritage.
  • Organise exhibitions, lectures, seminars, conferences, workshops, cultural programmes, educational tours and competitions.
  • Bring educational opportunities and knowledge-sharing to remote, rural and mountain communities.
  • Support lawful research on the traditions, livelihoods and social development of communities in Devbhoomi.
Libraries, research and publishing
  • Establish or support public libraries, reading rooms, archives, museums, documentation centres and digital libraries.
  • Conduct, commission and share educational, historical, linguistic, cultural, scientific and social research on Uttarakhand and India.
  • Publish, print, translate and distribute books, journals, research papers, reports and digital learning resources.
  • Preserve and digitise manuscripts, historical documents and oral histories, subject to law and permissions.
  • Widen access to learning through lawful websites, digital platforms and community learning initiatives.
Languages and knowledge systems
  • Promote Hindi, Sanskrit, the regional languages of Uttarakhand and other Indian languages through education, literature, translation and research.
  • Support the study of mathematics, science, technology, literature, arts, music, philosophy and ethics.
  • Encourage responsible, scholarly study of Indian knowledge systems, traditional knowledge, yoga and meditation.
  • Run language-learning programmes, literary events, reading competitions and writing workshops for children, students and adults.
Scholarships and educational assistance
  • Provide scholarships, fee assistance, books, uniforms, stationery and learning equipment.
  • Help students from economically weaker, rural, remote and underserved communities through fair and transparent selection criteria.
  • Support girls, women, persons with disabilities and other learners facing barriers.
  • Facilitate access to digital learning, educational technology and assistive devices.
  • Provide educational help during emergencies and disasters that interrupt learning.
Vocational education and skills
  • Provide vocational, technical, professional-readiness and skill-development programmes for youth, women and other deserving persons.
Social welfare and relief
  • Provide educational and related charitable assistance to children, women, senior citizens, persons with disabilities and disadvantaged persons.
  • Support access to learning, educational materials, life-skills programmes and lawful public-benefit services.
  • Undertake relief for people affected by poverty, disasters or emergencies, including books, clothing, food and essential supplies.
  • Support children whose education has been disrupted by poverty, displacement or disasters.
  • Undertake humanitarian activities that fit the Trust’s educational and charitable character.
Environment and Himalayan awareness
  • Promote environmental education and awareness of Himalayan ecology, biodiversity, forests, rivers, water resources and sustainable development.
  • Run programmes on tree plantation, water conservation, cleanliness, waste reduction and responsible use of natural resources.
  • Encourage environmental research and school and community awareness projects.
  • Promote understanding of disaster preparedness and climate awareness in the Himalayan region.
  • Take up practical environmental initiatives only in accordance with applicable laws, permissions and requirements.
Infrastructure and collaboration
  • Acquire, lease, construct, maintain and manage property, buildings and equipment needed for the Trust’s objects, subject to law.
  • Establish or support libraries, learning centres, resource facilities and research centres.
  • Collaborate with government bodies, schools, colleges, universities, research institutions, libraries, museums and charities.
  • Receive lawful donations, grants, gifts and endowments and apply them exclusively to the Trust’s objects, subject to donor restrictions.
  • Appoint teachers, trainers, researchers, employees, consultants and volunteers on lawful terms with conflict-of-interest safeguards.
Also: the Trust may undertake any other lawful activity that helps advance education, knowledge, research, cultural heritage, public welfare and the educational development of Devbhoomi Uttarakhand, as long as it stays consistent with its public charitable and educational character.
